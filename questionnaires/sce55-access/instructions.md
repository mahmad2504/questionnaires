You are requested to verify your access to SCE55 ETX, GitLab, and Artifactory by following the steps below.

## Step 1 – Access SCE55 ETX

Log in to your Arm W365 environment (AVD).

Open SCE55 ETX Url in browser https://etx.us-west-2.sce55.aws.arm.com/etx

Select your preferred OS type.

Log in using your 8-character Arm Unix ID (given at Arm onboarding) and Arm password.

Verify that you can successfully access the ETX environment.

## Step 2 – Verify GitLab Access

Open a browser within the ETX environment.

Go to [https://gitlab.geo.arm.com](https://gitlab.geo.arm.com). Make sure to use HTTPS.

Select SAML login.

If prompted, enter your Arm ID and password.

Verify that you can successfully log in.

## Step 3 – Verify Artifactory Access

Open a browser within the ETX environment.

Go to [https://artifactory.arm.com](https://artifactory.arm.com).

Select Log in with Azure.

No separate credentials are required.

Verify that you can successfully log in.

## What if access fails?

Not everyone is expected to have access to both GitLab and Artifactory at this stage. However, everyone should attempt to access both services.

If you are unable to log in, report which service failed so that the required permissions can be followed up.

## Important – Access Verification Only

Do not perform any actual work in these environments.

- Do not create, modify, or delete anything.
- Do not upload any files or data.
- Only verify that you can successfully log in.

## How to report

- **Done:** You attempted all three logins. Choose Success or Failed for each service. A failed service still counts as Done.
- **In Progress:** You have not finished the checks. Say where you are in the update.

A Done reply stays locked until an admin records a different status. You can submit again while the status is In Progress.
